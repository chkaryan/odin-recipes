Chocolate Moist Cake by nahlahrsl

Chocolate Moist Cake layered with Dark Chocolate Cremeux and Cream Cheese Fillings.

Home

Ingredients

Chocolate Moist Cake

  • 250g brown sugar
  • 160ml buttermilk
  • 160ml vegetable oil
  • 120g plain yogurt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 280g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250ml hot water
  • 1 tbsp instant coffee
  • 50g cocoa powder

Chocolate Cremeux

  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 120ml milk
  • 120ml whipping cream
  • 2 eggs + 1 yolk
  • 25g or 2 tbsp sugar

  • 250g cream cheese
  • 15g cocoa powder
  • 30g powdered sugar
  • 120ml cold whipping cream

Instructions

  1. Prepare 200g of dark chocolate in a bowl.
  2. Put in 2 whole eggs, 1 egg yolk, 120ml milk, 120ml whipping cream, and 15g sugar in a pot.
  3. Cook to simmer on low heat while stirring frequently.
  4. Pour the mixture into the 200g dark chocolate and let it sit for 5 minutes. Stir until silky smooth.
  5. Cover with cling wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  6. In a mixing bowl, combine 250g cream cheese, 1 rsp vanilla, 15g cocoa powder and 30g powdered sugar.
  7. Mix until smooth and pour 120ml cold whipping cream.
  8. Add in the cold cremeux and mix again until well combined.
  9. Cover with cling wrap and refrigerate again for at least 1 hour.
  10. In a bowl, add in 250g brown sugar, 160ml buttermilk, 160ml vegetable oil, 120g plain yogurt, 2 large eggs, 1 tsp vanilla extract. Whisk until well combined.
  11. Sift in 280g all-purpose flour, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp baking powder, 2 tsp baking soda. Whisk again until well coorperated.
  12. Measure 250ml hot water in a measuring cup and add 1tbsp instant coffee and 50g cocoa powder. Stir until smooth.
  13. Pour the coffee and cocoa powder into the mixture and whisk.
  14. Pour the whisked mixture into a 13"x9" pan and bake in a preheated oven at 160 C for 30-35 minutes.
  15. Let it cool completely and cut into 4"x8" size pieces.
  16. Layer the cake and cream. Repeat.
  17. Refrigerate for at least 4-5 hours or overnight before serving.
  18. Add coarse salt on top and serve!
