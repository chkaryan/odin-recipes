Cheesy garlic Rolls by Erin's Cozy Kitchen

Pillowy cinnamon-roll style garlic buns with rich butter swirls and bakery-level texture.

Home

Ingredients

Dough

  • 360 g bread flour
  • 25 g granulated sugar
  • 6 g salt
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 185 g warm milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 65 g unsalted butter (softened)

Garlic Butter Filling

  • 90 g butter (softened, use salted or unsalted based on personal preference)
  • 30 g mayo
  • 50 g minced garlic (use a whole head of roasted garlic for deeper flavour)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp parsley (finely chopped)
  • 250 g mozzarella cheese (shredded)

Instructions

Make the Dough

  1. In the bowl of a stand mixer (or lagre mixing bowl), combine bread flour, sugar, salt, instant yeast, warm whole milk (about 105-110 F), and egg.
  2. Mix until a rough dough forms and no dry flour remains.
  3. Once a cohesive dough ball forms, add softened unsalted butter.
  4. Knead until the butter is fully incorporated and continue kneading 8-12 minuts until dough is smooth and elastic and passes the windowpane test (stretch a small piece thin enough to see light through without tearing).

First Rise

  1. Roll dough into tight ball and transfer to a lightly greased bowl.
  2. Cover and let rise at room temperature for about an hour. The dough should double in size.

Prepare the Garlic Butter Filling

In a bowl, mix softened butter, mayonnaise, minced garlic, oregano, and finely chopped parsley. Mix until smooth and spreadable.

Roll and Shape

  1. Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface.
  2. Roll into a rectangle about 10 x 14 inches.
  3. Spread filling evenly over the surface, leaving a 1/4-inch border along far short edge. Sprinkle over your shredded mozzarella cheese.
  4. Starting from the short side closest to you (not the side with the border), roll snugly into a log.
  5. Slice into 9 rolls using a serrated knife.
  6. Place into a gresed 9x9 inch pan, leaving slight space between rolls.

Second Proof

Cover let rise 45-60 minutes at room temperature. Note: Rolls should look puffy and increased in size by about 70–80%. A gentle poke should slowly spring back halfway.

Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F. Optional: Sprinkle a little more mozzarella cheese over the rolls before baking.
  2. Bake for 20-25 min. The rolls are done when they are golden brown and reach and internal temp of 190-195 F.
  3. Brush with melted butter mixed with finely chopped parsley for aesthetic shine.
  4. Let the rolls cool slightly and serve!
