Honey Garlic Chicken by zachs.foods

Easy dinner recipe that's better than takeout.

Home

Ingredients

For the Meat:

  • 1lb chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp canola oil
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch (enough to coat)

For the Sauce:

  • 1,5 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 tbsp green onion whites

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with enough salt and pepper.
  2. Add a the canola oil and cornstarch just enough to coat the chicken.
  3. Mix together.
  4. In a seperate bowl, add the sauce ingredients together and set it aside.
  5. On a hot pan, add the chicken and cook until golden brown.
  6. Remove the cooked chicken out and with the same pan, add the green onion whites and a tablespoon of butter.
  7. After the butter melted, add the prepared sauce in to the pan.
  8. Then, add the chicken and coat them with the sauce.
  9. Put some rice in a bowl, add the chicken, garnish with sesame seeds and green onion, and serve!
